History

Founded in 2018 in Gqeberha, the Trust was formed by the local marine biologists to protect Marine Eco System coastal ecosystems and African Penguin populations.
The Trust was established to directly combat rapid environmental challenges in the Nelson Mandela Bay area, incliuding the marine plastics pollution, habitat and coatal urban runoff.

Mission Statement

To protect Gqeberha's marine life through community education like to empower the local schools and youth groups also Eastern Cape communities with pratical environment knowlwdge,
we also conduct interactive workshops, the marines awareness campaigns and guided educational sessions focused on the Marine Eco System, recovery programmes, and active coastal clean ups.

Vision Statement

To establish the Marine Eco System a world leading, sustainably managed marine sanctuary. We also build a strong local and international research partnerships,
ensuring every conservation strategy implemented in the Eco System Marine isdriven by the dat, innovation and long term suistanability.
